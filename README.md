FROSTLINE is a top-down open-world crime game set in Ironhold, a snowbound industrial rust-belt city on a frozen lake. Drive the ploughed ice road across the lake, smuggle cargo between the harbor docks and the safehouse, steal cars, and outrun the police through falling snow at night, in an amber-and-navy palette.

The campaign runs eight story missions, from a first getaway drive to a five-star vault heist with a helicopter overhead and roadblocks on the ice. Between jobs, spend your cash at the garage (tuned engines, armor, studded ice tires), the gun shop, and the outfitters, climb respect ranks from Rookie to King of the Lake, and tune the radio across three stations. Progress saves to slots, and the safehouse heals and saves.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```

## How to play

- **WASD / arrow keys** — drive and walk
- **E** — enter/exit a car, interact (G also works)
- **Space** — shoot on foot, handbrake while driving
- **M** — missions menu
- **R** — cycle radio stations (FROST FM 88.1, IRONHOLD ROCK 94.7, NORTHLIGHT 101.3)
- **P or Esc** — pause
- **1 / 2 / 3** — save in a slot
- **X** — toggle sound
- **Enter** — confirm, **T** — title screen
